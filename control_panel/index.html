First-run tips

• Your motions may feel mirrored or sideways on the first live run — that's the camera axis mapping, and it's expected. Tell Claude how it feels (e.g. “pushing forward moves it left”) to have the signs flipped in teleop/retarget.py.
• Camera framing: the camera must see your shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand. Use the camera view above — “pose” and “hand” both turn green when it can track you. Aim the camera down / sit back until they do.
• The robot view is rendered in software (no GPU) at ~5 fps — the physics and recorded data run at full rate regardless.
• You need about 50 successful demonstrations before Train is worth running.
